Hi, I’m Angelina Golt

I grew up on the Black Sea, where Greek, Ukrainian and Jewish cooking all met at one table, and that’s where I learned what matters most to me: long tables full of food to share, flavour over pretty plates, and that feeling when you want to sneak the meze home with you. Later I became a chef at Brutto in London and in Italy. One of my recipes is featured in Cuisines of Odesa: A Ukrainian Cookbook.

For me, the best compliment is an empty plate.

Catering
